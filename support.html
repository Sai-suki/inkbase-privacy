Frequently asked questions

How do I turn my handwriting into Anki cards?

  1. Write on the canvas.
  2. Select the content with the Lasso or Marquee tool.
  3. Tap Make card and choose a deck.
  4. Mark the regions to hide with the Cover tool.
  5. Open the Export Center and tap Anki Package (.apkg).
  6. Import the file into Anki desktop or AnkiMobile.

Why does one card produce several notes in Anki?

A card with N occlusion regions is exported as 1 + N notes: one full image card plus one note per hidden region. This matches Anki's native "Hide One, Guess One" behaviour, so the remaining regions stay visible as context while you recall the hidden one.

Do I need an Anki account or the Anki add-on?

No. The exported .apkg is a standard Anki package and imports directly — no plugin needed, no account required.

Where is my data stored?

Inside the app sandbox on your device, encrypted with AES-GCM. There is no account, no upload and no third-party SDK. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does it sync between devices?

iCloud sync is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, ordinary notebooks sync through your own Apple ID. Notebooks marked as audited are never uploaded, and audio recordings and original PDFs never sync.

I bought the unlock but it is not active after reinstalling

Open Settings → Purchase → Restore Purchases and sign in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. The entitlement unlocks .apkg export, iCloud sync and audited notebooks.

What does the free version include?

Everything except unlimited exports: unlimited notebooks, unlimited handwriting, flashcards and review. .apkg export is limited to 3 per month on the free tier.

I forgot the passcode of an audited notebook

The passcode is stored only on your device and cannot be recovered by us. After 5 incorrect attempts, each further attempt waits twice as long as the previous one, capped at 15 minutes. If the passcode is lost, the notebook's audit trail cannot be continued — export the notebook content from another notebook if you still have access to it.

How do I back up my notes?

Use Export to save a copy as PDF, image or Anki package. With iCloud sync off, deleting the app also deletes its data, so export anything you want to keep.

Something looks wrong with the drawing

Try closing and reopening the note. If the problem repeats, email us with the note's page count, the tool you were using and your device model.
