Inkbase — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026  ·  Effective: 21 November 2026  ·  中文版

In short: Inkbase collects no personal information and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. Your notes stay on your own device by default. iCloud sync is optional and only turns on if you choose it, and audited notebooks are never uploaded.

1. Who we are

Inkbase ("the app") is a local-first handwriting app for iPad and iPhone, offering handwritten notes, PDF annotation, voice memos and flashcard review.

2. What we do not collect

The app does not collect, upload, sell or share any user data. Specifically:

  • No identity data — no registration, no phone number, email or name required
  • No note content — note text, handwriting strokes and flashcard records are never uploaded
  • No recordings — audio files are stored only in the local app sandbox
  • No PDF content — imported documents are parsed on device only
  • No device identifiers — no IDFA, no UUID reporting
  • No third-party SDKs — no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting

3. Where your data is stored

All data lives inside the app sandbox on your device:

  • Note database: the app's Documents directory
  • Audio recordings: the Inkbase/Audio subdirectory
  • Imported PDFs: the Inkbase/Documents subdirectory

This means:

  • Deleting the app deletes all data (unless you have turned on iCloud sync, below)
  • With iCloud sync off, data does not move to a new device or survive a reinstall — use the in-app export feature to keep your own backup

4. iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

The app does not connect to the network and uploads nothing by default. iCloud sync is an optional feature you turn on yourself, used to keep notes in step across your devices.

  • Off by default. If you do not enable it, your data never leaves your device.
  • When enabled, the text, handwriting strokes, PDF annotations and flashcard review records of ordinary notebooks are stored in the iCloud private database under your own Apple ID. That data is hosted by Apple; we cannot access or read it.
  • Notebooks marked "audited" are never uploaded, whether or not sync is on. If a notebook was synced before being marked audited, the old cloud copy is deleted.
  • Audio recordings and original imported PDFs do not participate in iCloud sync and remain on device only.
  • You can turn sync off at any time in the app settings; local data is kept, and the cloud copy simply stops updating.

If you separately enable iOS device backup in iCloud, the app sandbox may be included in that backup. Such backups are controlled by Apple and your Apple ID; the app cannot access them.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not collect usage statistics or behavioural analytics
  • We do not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs or tracking code
  • We have no user account system; no phone number, email or name is collected
  • We do not sell or share any note content

6. How speech transcription works

Transcription uses the system's on-device recognition capability:

  • Audio data does not leave your device
  • We do not receive, store or forward any speech content
  • If your device or region does not support on-device recognition, the app says so; the recording itself is still saved locally in full

7. Permissions

The app requests permissions only where a real feature needs them:

  • Microphone — for voice memos. Used only while you hold the record button.
  • Speech recognition — to transcribe recordings, on device only.
  • Photo library (read) — to insert an image into a note; only the image you pick is read.
  • Photo library (write) — to save an exported note as an image.
  • Camera — to photograph paper material into a note.

All permissions can be revoked in system settings at any time; the related feature stops working, and nothing else is affected.

8. Children

The app contains no advertising, no paid-push mechanics, no lotteries and no stranger social features, and does not target content or recommendations at minors. Because the app collects no personal information at all, it collects no personal information from children. If a minor uses the app, we suggest doing so with a guardian nearby.

9. Your rights

Because your data lives entirely on your device, you have complete control:

  • Access — everything is visible in the app
  • Change — edit at any time
  • Delete — deleted items go to Trash, are cleared after 30 days, or can be erased immediately
  • Export — Markdown, plain text and Anki packages; your data leaves with you
  • Revoke permissions — Settings → Inkbase

Since we never collected your personal information, there is no "request deletion from us" step — the data was always in your hands.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we update it in the app's Privacy & Compliance page and change the date at the top of this page. Material changes are announced prominently in the app.
